# Lance Lunceford Strategies

Make a personal website for Lance Lunceford. There should be a homepage, about lance page, a public policy and economic development page, a business ventures page, and musician page, and finally a contact page. The about page will have info about him. The public and econ page will tell about his work as a poltician, EDC chairman, etc. and link to those organizations. The business ventures will tell about his role in finance & with his business The Lunceford Group & startups. The musician page will tell about his career as a professional trombonist. The homepage headline should communicate long-term governance, economic strength, and strategic leadership.

Avoid anything trendy, overly animated, or casual. The site should feel like it represents someone who influences policy, capital, and regional outcomes.

It should give very high class, the classics, while also feeling like someone very innovative. A clear blend of the most important historical figures and traditional values alongside true innovation for the future. 
If done correctly, your site will:

Elevate you beyond “local figure”

Position you as a regional strategist with national-level thinking

Serve as a hub for policy, capital, and influence
